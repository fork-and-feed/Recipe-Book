Sourdough Grilled Cheese

A crispy, golden grilled cheese made with tangy sourdough bread and plenty of melted cheese.

← Back to Home
Prep: 5 min Cook: 8–10 min Serves: 2 Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 4 slices sourdough bread
  • 2 tbsp softened butter or mayonnaise
  • 1½ cups shredded cheese, or 4–6 slices cheese
  • ¼ tsp garlic powder (optional)
  • Freshly ground black pepper (optional)

Cheese Suggestions

  • Cheddar
  • Mozzarella
  • Monterey Jack
  • Havarti
  • Gouda
  • Swiss

Instructions

1. Assemble the Sandwiches

  1. Spread butter or mayonnaise evenly over one side of each slice of sourdough bread.
  2. Place two slices of bread buttered-side down on a cutting board.
  3. Divide the cheese evenly between the two slices.
  4. Sprinkle with garlic powder and black pepper, if desired.
  5. Top with the remaining bread, buttered-side facing up.

2. Cook the Grilled Cheese

  1. Heat a large skillet over medium-low heat.
  2. Cook the sandwiches for 3–5 minutes, or until the bottoms are golden brown.
  3. Flip carefully and cook another 3–5 minutes until the second side is golden brown and the cheese is completely melted.
  4. If the bread is browning before the cheese melts, cover the skillet with a lid for the final 1–2 minutes.

3. Serve

  1. Allow the sandwiches to rest for 1 minute before slicing.
  2. Serve warm with tomato soup, pickles, or a simple salad.

Tips

  • Medium-low heat allows the cheese to melt before the sourdough becomes too dark.
  • Shredded cheese melts more quickly and evenly than thick slices.
  • For thick-cut sourdough, gently press the sandwich with a spatula while it cooks.
  • A combination of sharp cheddar and mozzarella provides great flavor and an excellent cheese pull.
