Teriyaki-Garlic Chicken Stir-Fry

Tender chicken, crisp broccoli, and colorful bell peppers tossed in a sweet and savory homemade teriyaki-garlic glaze, served over fluffy rice.

← Back to Home
Prep: 15 min Marinate: 10 min Cook: 10–15 min Serves: 3–4 Stir-Fry

Ingredients

Main Stir-Fry

  • 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 2–3 cups broccoli florets
  • 1–2 bell peppers (any color), cut into bite-sized chunks
  • 2–3 cups cooked rice, for serving
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil (peanut or canola), divided

Chicken Marinade

  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 tsp sesame oil

Savory Teriyaki-Garlic Sauce

  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp water or chicken broth
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar or honey
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar or apple cider vinegar
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 tsp cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp chili garlic sauce or Sriracha (optional, for heat)

Instructions

1. Marinate the Chicken

Place the bite-sized chicken pieces in a medium mixing bowl.

Add the soy sauce, cornstarch, and sesame oil. Toss thoroughly until the chicken is evenly coated.

Let the chicken marinate for 10 minutes while preparing the vegetables and sauce.

2. Prepare the Teriyaki-Garlic Sauce

In a small bowl, combine the soy sauce, water or chicken broth, brown sugar or honey, rice vinegar, minced garlic, grated ginger, and cornstarch.

Add the optional chili garlic sauce or Sriracha for a spicy variation.

Whisk until the sugar dissolves and the cornstarch is completely incorporated.

Set aside until ready to use.

3. Sear the Chicken

Heat 1 tbsp cooking oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat.

Add the marinated chicken in a single layer, working in batches if necessary to avoid overcrowding.

Allow the chicken to sear undisturbed for 2 minutes to develop a golden crust.

Stir-fry for another 2–3 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through and reaches an internal temperature of 165°F.

Transfer the cooked chicken to a plate and set aside.

4. Stir-Fry the Vegetables

Add the remaining 1 tbsp cooking oil to the same skillet.

Add the broccoli florets and chopped bell peppers.

Stir-fry over high heat for approximately 3 minutes, until the vegetables are vibrant and crisp-tender.

If the broccoli needs additional cooking, add a small splash of water and cook briefly until it reaches your preferred tenderness.

5. Add the Chicken & Thicken the Sauce

Return the cooked chicken to the skillet with the broccoli and bell peppers.

Give the prepared teriyaki-garlic sauce another quick whisk to redistribute any settled cornstarch.

Pour the sauce evenly over the chicken and vegetables.

Toss continuously over medium-high to high heat for 1–2 minutes, until the sauce begins to bubble and thickens into a glossy glaze.

Continue tossing until the chicken and vegetables are evenly coated.

6. Serve

Spoon the teriyaki-garlic chicken and vegetables over freshly cooked, steaming rice.

Drizzle any remaining sauce from the skillet over the top and serve immediately.

Tips

  • For tender chicken: The cornstarch marinade helps create a lightly coated exterior and keeps the chicken tender during high-heat cooking.
  • For extra spice: Add chili garlic sauce or Sriracha to the teriyaki sauce, adjusting the amount to taste.
  • For crisp vegetables: Cook over high heat and avoid overcrowding the skillet.
  • For a thicker glaze: Let the sauce bubble for an additional 30–60 seconds while stirring continuously.
  • For a sweeter sauce: Use honey instead of brown sugar for a slightly different sweetness and finish.
  • For easy meal preparation: Prepare the rice, chop the vegetables, and mix the sauce before cooking the chicken.
