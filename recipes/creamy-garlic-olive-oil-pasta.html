Creamy Garlic and Olive Oil Pasta

A silky garlic pasta made by blending tender boiled garlic with starchy pasta water and extra virgin olive oil into a naturally creamy sauce—without cream or cheese.

← Back to Home
Prep: 5 min Cook: 15–20 min Serves: 4–5 Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 454 g spaghetti, bucatini, or linguine
  • 55–60 g whole garlic cloves, peeled (about 1 medium head)
  • 3/8 cup high-quality extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 cup reserved hot starchy pasta water
  • 15 g salt (for the pasta water)
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • Red pepper flakes, to taste (optional)
  • 2–3 tbsp finely chopped fresh parsley or basil (optional)

Instructions

1. Boil the Garlic

  1. Place the peeled garlic cloves in a small saucepan and cover with water.
  2. Bring to a gentle boil and cook for 10–12 minutes, or until the garlic is completely tender and easily pierced with a fork.
  3. Drain well and set aside.

2. Cook the Pasta

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and add the salt.
  2. Add the pasta and cook until about 1–2 minutes before al dente.
  3. Before draining, reserve 1 cup of the hot starchy pasta water.
  4. Drain the pasta without rinsing.

3. Blend the Garlic Sauce

  1. Place the boiled garlic and the full 1 cup of hot pasta water into a blender or tall blending container.
  2. Blend until completely smooth.
  3. With the blender running on low, slowly stream in the olive oil until the sauce becomes pale, smooth, and fully emulsified.
  4. Caution: Hot liquids expand when blended. Follow your blender manufacturer's safety instructions and allow steam to escape as recommended.

4. Finish the Pasta

  1. Pour the garlic sauce into the empty pasta pot or a large skillet.
  2. Add the drained pasta and cook over low heat, tossing continuously for 1–2 minutes until the sauce lightly thickens and coats every strand.
  3. Remove from the heat and stir in black pepper, red pepper flakes, and fresh herbs, if using.
  4. Taste before adding additional salt, as the pasta water may already provide enough seasoning.
  5. Let the pasta rest for 1 minute before serving. The sauce will continue to thicken slightly.
  6. Serve immediately.

Optional Additions

Roasted Cherry Tomatoes

  • 250 g cherry tomatoes
  • 1–2 tsp olive oil
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
  2. Toss the tomatoes with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  3. Roast for 15–20 minutes until blistered, softened, and beginning to burst.
  4. Fold the tomatoes and their juices into the finished pasta just before serving.

Crispy Bacon

  • 150 g bacon, diced or chopped
  1. Cook the bacon over medium heat until crisp.
  2. Drain on paper towels.
  3. Fold the bacon into the finished pasta immediately before serving.
  4. For extra flavor, stir up to 1 tbsp of the rendered bacon fat into the garlic sauce.
  5. If using bacon, reduce the salt in the pasta water slightly and season the finished dish to taste.

Storage & Reheating

  • Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
  • Reheat gently over low heat with a small splash of water, tossing frequently until the sauce becomes creamy again.
