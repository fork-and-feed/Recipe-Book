Mustard Sauce

A creamy Béchamel-based mustard sauce finished with Dijon, lemon, and optional fresh parsley.

← Back to Home
Prep: 5 min Cook: 5 min Yield: ~2 cups Classic Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1 recipe prepared Béchamel Sauce, hot
  • 2–3 tbsp Dijon mustard (or whole-grain mustard)
  • 1 tsp whole-grain mustard (optional, for texture)
  • 1 tsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped (optional)

Instructions

1. Prepare the Base

Prepare the standard Béchamel Sauce and remove the saucepan from the heat.

2. Add the Mustard

Whisk in the Dijon mustard and whole-grain mustard, if using, until fully incorporated into the warm sauce.

Important: Do not boil the sauce after adding the mustard to preserve its flavor.

3. Finish & Season

Stir in the fresh lemon juice and chopped parsley, if using. Taste and adjust the salt and pepper as needed.

Serve immediately over chicken, pork, sausages, or fish.
