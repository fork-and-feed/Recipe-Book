Spicy Honey Garlic Sauce

A quick sweet and spicy honey mustard sauce with Dijon, mayo, mustard powder, and chili oil.

← Back to Home
Prep: 2 min Rest: 5–10 min No Cooking Quick Sauce

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 1/2 tsp mustard powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp chili oil

Instructions

  1. Add the honey, mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, mustard powder, and chili oil to a small bowl.
  2. Whisk until completely smooth and evenly combined.
  3. Let the sauce rest for 5–10 minutes before serving to allow the flavors to blend.
