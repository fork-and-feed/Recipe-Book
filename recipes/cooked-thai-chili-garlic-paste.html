Cooked Thai Chili Garlic Paste

A rich, aromatic chili paste that freezes beautifully and adds instant heat and flavor to stir-fries, soups, and sauces.

← Back to Home
Prep: 10 min Cook: 5–8 min Yield: ~1 cup Freezer Friendly

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Thai chilies, stems removed
  • 5–6 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional)
  • 1–2 tbsp vinegar or lime juice
  • 2–3 tbsp neutral oil (canola, avocado, or vegetable)

Instructions

  1. Blend: Combine Thai chilies, garlic, salt, sugar (if using), and vinegar or lime juice in a blender or food processor. Blend until smooth or slightly chunky, depending on your preference.
  2. Cook: Heat the oil in a skillet or saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the chili mixture and cook for 5–8 minutes, stirring frequently, until the raw garlic smell disappears and the paste becomes fragrant.
  3. Cool: Remove from the heat and allow the paste to cool completely.
  4. Store: Transfer to a clean, airtight jar.

Storage

  • Refrigerator: 1–2 weeks
  • Freezer: Up to 6 months (freeze in ice cube trays or small containers for easy portions)

How to Use

Sauté 1–2 teaspoons in a little oil before adding meat, vegetables, noodles, rice, soups, or sauces to build a rich, spicy flavor base.
