Watermelon, Cucumber & Basil Salad

A refreshing summer salad with sweet watermelon, crisp cucumber, fresh basil, and a honey-balsamic dressing.

← Back to Home
Prep: 15 min Chill: 10–15 min Serves: 4–6 No Cooking

Ingredients

Salad

  • 6 cups seedless watermelon, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 1 English cucumber, sliced into half-moons
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil leaves, torn

Dressing

  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp flaky sea salt (or to taste)
  • 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Place the watermelon and cucumber in a large serving bowl.
  2. Add the torn basil leaves.
  3. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, balsamic vinegar, honey, salt, and pepper until the honey is fully dissolved.
  4. Pour the dressing over the salad and gently toss until everything is evenly coated.
  5. Refrigerate for 10–15 minutes before serving, or serve immediately.

Storage

  • Best enjoyed the day it is made.
  • Leftovers can be refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 1 day, though the watermelon will naturally release juice over time.

Tips

  • Use ripe, chilled watermelon for the best flavor.
  • Add the basil just before serving to keep it fresh and vibrant.
  • Taste the dressing before adding it to the salad. If you'd like it sweeter, whisk in an additional 1–2 tsp honey.
  • Finish with a pinch of flaky sea salt just before serving to enhance the sweetness of the watermelon.
