Béchamel Sauce

A classic creamy white sauce made with butter, flour, warm milk, and a touch of nutmeg.

← Back to Home
Prep: 5 min Cook: 10 min Yield: ~2 cups Classic Sauce

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp (30 g) unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp (30 g) all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups (480 mL) whole milk, warmed
  • 1/8 tsp freshly grated nutmeg
  • Salt and white pepper (or black pepper), to taste

Instructions

1. Melt the Butter

Melt the butter in a heavy-bottomed saucepan over medium-low heat. Do not let it brown.

2. Make the Roux

Add the flour and whisk constantly for 1–2 minutes until the mixture is smooth and bubbly. This cooks out the raw flour taste while keeping the roux pale.

3. Incorporate the Milk

Slowly pour in the warm milk in a steady stream, whisking vigorously to prevent lumps.

4. Thicken the Sauce

Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer over medium heat, stirring frequently. Cook for 5–8 minutes, until the sauce thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon.

5. Season & Finish

Remove the saucepan from the heat. Stir in the nutmeg, salt, and white or black pepper. Taste and adjust the seasoning as needed.
