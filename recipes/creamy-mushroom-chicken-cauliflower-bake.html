Creamy Mushroom Chicken & Cauliflower Bake

A comforting oven-baked casserole with tender chicken thighs, cauliflower, creamy mushroom sauce, and melted cheese, with an optional buttery cracker topping.

← Back to Home
Prep: 10 min Cook: 40–45 min Serves: 4 Oven: 375°F

Ingredients

Chicken & Cauliflower

  • 1 1/2–2 lb chicken thighs, boneless and skinless or bone-in
  • 1 head cauliflower, cut into bite-sized florets
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Creamy Mushroom Sauce

  • 1 can (10.5 oz) condensed cream of mushroom soup
  • 1/2 cup sour cream (or milk)
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth (or water)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced (or 1/2 tsp garlic powder)
  • 1 cup shredded Cheddar or Swiss cheese, divided
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp dried thyme
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Optional Cracker Topping

  • 1/2 cup crushed Ritz crackers
  • 1 tbsp butter, melted

Instructions

1. Preheat the Oven

Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).

Lightly grease a 9 × 13-inch baking dish to prevent sticking.

2. Prepare the Creamy Mushroom Sauce

In a medium mixing bowl, combine the condensed cream of mushroom soup, sour cream, chicken broth, minced garlic, onion powder, dried thyme, salt, and black pepper.

Add 1/2 cup of the shredded cheese, reserving the remaining cheese for the topping.

Whisk until the mixture is smooth and evenly combined.

3. Arrange the Chicken & Cauliflower

Spread the cauliflower florets evenly across the bottom of the prepared baking dish.

Season the chicken thighs with salt and freshly ground black pepper.

Nestle the seasoned chicken thighs among the cauliflower, arranging everything in an even layer.

4. Add the Mushroom Sauce

Pour the prepared mushroom sauce evenly over the chicken and cauliflower.

Use a spoon or spatula to distribute the sauce, ensuring the ingredients are well coated.

5. Cover & Bake

Cover the baking dish tightly with aluminum foil.

Transfer to the preheated oven and bake for 30 minutes.

6. Add the Cheese & Optional Topping

Carefully remove the baking dish from the oven and discard the foil.

Sprinkle the remaining 1/2 cup shredded cheese evenly over the casserole.

If using the cracker topping, combine the crushed Ritz crackers with the melted butter in a small bowl.

Scatter the buttered cracker crumbs evenly over the cheese.

7. Finish Baking Uncovered

Return the uncovered baking dish to the oven.

Bake for another 10–15 minutes, until the cheese is melted, the sauce is bubbling, and the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C).

If needed, continue baking until the chicken is fully cooked and the cauliflower is tender.

8. Rest & Serve

Remove the casserole from the oven and allow it to rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Spoon the creamy mushroom sauce over the chicken and cauliflower when serving.

Tips

  • Boneless vs. bone-in chicken: Bone-in thighs may require additional cooking time. Always check the internal temperature before serving.
  • For a richer sauce: Use sour cream instead of milk.
  • Cheese options: Cheddar provides a sharper flavor, while Swiss creates a milder, nutty finish.
  • For a crispy topping: Use the buttered Ritz cracker mixture and bake uncovered until lightly golden.
  • For tender cauliflower: Cut the florets into evenly sized pieces to encourage consistent cooking.
