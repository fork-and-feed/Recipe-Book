Chicken & Bell Pepper Egg Noodle Stir-Fry

A quick and flavorful stir-fry with tender chicken, crisp bell peppers, and egg noodles tossed in a sweet and savory garlic-soy glaze.

← Back to Home
Prep: 10 min Cook: 10 min Serves: 2–3 Stir-Fry

Ingredients

Main Stir-Fry

  • 1 package (300 g) Blue Dragon Medium Egg Noodles
  • 1 lb (450 g) boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 2 large bell peppers (any color), chopped into 1-inch pieces
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, minced or grated (optional)
  • 1 1/2 tbsp neutral cooking oil (vegetable, canola, or avocado), divided
  • Green onions or sesame seeds, for garnish (optional)

Stir-Fry Sauce

  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey or brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp warm water or chicken broth
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar or apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp cornstarch
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes or sriracha (optional, for heat)

Instructions

1. Cook the Noodles

Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add the Blue Dragon Medium Egg Noodles and cook for 3–4 minutes, or according to package instructions, until just tender.

Drain thoroughly and toss with a small splash of oil to prevent the noodles from sticking together. Set aside.

2. Prepare the Sauce

In a small bowl, combine the soy sauce, honey or brown sugar, warm water or chicken broth, vinegar, sesame oil, cornstarch, and optional red pepper flakes or sriracha.

Whisk until completely smooth and free of lumps. Set aside.

3. Sear the Chicken

Heat 1 tbsp cooking oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat.

Add the cubed chicken in a single layer. Allow it to cook undisturbed for 3–4 minutes, until golden on the bottom.

Flip the chicken and continue cooking until fully cooked through and the internal temperature reaches 165°F.

Transfer the chicken to a plate and set aside.

4. Stir-Fry the Vegetables

Add the remaining 1/2 tbsp cooking oil to the same skillet.

Add the chopped bell peppers, minced garlic, and optional ginger.

Stir-fry over medium-high heat for 2–3 minutes, until the peppers are brightly colored and crisp-tender.

5. Combine the Chicken & Noodles

Return the cooked chicken to the skillet with the bell peppers.

Add the drained egg noodles and gently toss everything together until evenly distributed.

6. Glaze & Serve

Give the prepared sauce another quick whisk to redistribute the cornstarch.

Pour the sauce evenly over the chicken, noodles, and vegetables.

Toss continuously over medium-high heat for 1–2 minutes, until the sauce bubbles and thickens into a glossy glaze that coats everything evenly.

Remove from the heat and garnish with sliced green onions or sesame seeds, if desired.

Serve immediately while hot.

Tips

  • Avoid overcooking the noodles: Cook them until just tender, as they will continue cooking briefly when tossed with the sauce.
  • For extra heat: Increase the red pepper flakes or sriracha to taste.
  • For a thicker glaze: Allow the sauce to bubble for an additional 30–60 seconds, stirring continuously.
  • For crisp vegetables: Keep the skillet hot and avoid overcooking the bell peppers.
