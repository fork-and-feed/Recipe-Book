Pickled Sweet Onions (Apple Cider Vinegar)

Quick refrigerator pickled sweet onions with a lightly sweet apple cider vinegar brine.

← Back to Home
Prep: 15 min Soak: 30–60 min Ready: 24–48 hrs Refrigerator Pickles

Ingredients

Onion Soak

  • 2 large sweet onions (about 4 cups thinly sliced)
  • 2 cups cold water
  • 1 tbsp pickling salt or non-iodized salt

Pickling Brine

  • 1 cup apple cider vinegar (5% acidity)
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 tbsp sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 1 tsp pickling salt or non-iodized salt
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns (optional)
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds (optional)
  • 1 bay leaf (optional)
  • Chili flakes (optional)

Instructions

  1. Thinly slice the sweet onions.
  2. Dissolve 1 tbsp salt in 2 cups cold water. Add the onions and soak for 30–60 minutes.
  3. Drain the onions and rinse briefly with cold water.
  4. Pack the onions into a clean jar, leaving a little space at the top.
  5. In a small saucepan, combine the apple cider vinegar, water, sugar, salt, and any optional spices. Warm gently until the sugar and salt dissolve. Do not boil.
  6. Pour the warm brine over the onions until they are completely submerged.
  7. Allow to cool to room temperature, then seal the jar and refrigerate.
  8. For the best flavor, wait 24–48 hours before serving. The flavor continues to improve during the first week.

Storage

  • Keep refrigerated.
  • Best quality within 2–3 months.
  • Keep the onions fully submerged in the brine and always use a clean utensil when serving.

Serving Ideas

  • Burgers and sandwiches
  • Tacos and wraps
  • Salads
  • Charcuterie boards
  • Grilled meats
