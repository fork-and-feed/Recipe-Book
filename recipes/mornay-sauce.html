Mornay Sauce

A classic French cheese sauce made from Béchamel with Gruyère and Parmesan.

← Back to Home
Prep: 5 min Cook: 5 min Yield: ~2 1/2 cups Classic Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1 recipe prepared Béchamel Sauce, hot
  • 1 cup (100 g) Gruyère or Swiss cheese, shredded
  • 1/4 cup (25 g) Parmesan cheese, finely grated
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter (optional, for gloss)
  • Pinch of cayenne pepper (optional)

Instructions

1. Prepare the Base

Prepare the standard Béchamel Sauce and remove the saucepan from the heat.

2. Add the Cheese

Add the Gruyère and Parmesan to the hot sauce in small batches, whisking gently after each addition until completely melted and smooth.

Important: Do not boil the sauce after adding the cheese, as excessive heat can cause the sauce to become grainy.

3. Finish & Season

Stir in the additional tablespoon of butter and a pinch of cayenne pepper, if desired. Taste and adjust the salt and pepper as needed.

Serve immediately while hot and creamy.
