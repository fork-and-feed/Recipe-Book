Immersion Blender Hollandaise Sauce

A quick, rich Hollandaise made with egg yolks, lemon, and hot butter using an immersion blender for an easy, creamy emulsion.

← Back to Home
Prep: 5 min Cook: 2 min Yield: ~1 cup Immersion Blender

Ingredients

  • 3 large egg yolks
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tsp water
  • 1/2 cup (115 g) unsalted butter, melted and hot
  • Pinch of cayenne pepper
  • Pinch of salt, to taste

Instructions

1. Combine the Base Ingredients

Add the egg yolks, lemon juice, water, and salt to a tall, narrow jar or container that just fits the head of your immersion blender.

2. Blend the Base

Insert the immersion blender all the way to the bottom of the jar. Pulse for a few seconds until the yolk mixture is pale and completely combined.

3. Stream in the Hot Butter

With the immersion blender running continuously on high and held at the bottom of the jar, slowly pour in the hot melted butter in a thin, steady stream.

4. Emulsify

As the sauce begins to thicken and emulsify, about 30 seconds, slowly pull the blender head upward through the mixture until all of the butter is incorporated and the sauce is thick, smooth, and creamy.

5. Season & Serve

Stir in the cayenne pepper. Taste and adjust the salt or lemon juice as needed.

Serve immediately.
