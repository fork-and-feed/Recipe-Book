Sesame Peanut Noodles with Chicken

Quick noodles tossed with chicken in a creamy sesame-peanut sauce, topped with crisp cucumber and your favorite garnishes.

← Back to Home
Prep: 10 min Cook: 10 min Serves: 2–3 Quick Meal

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked chicken, shredded or diced (rotisserie or leftover cooked chicken breast/thigh)
  • 8 oz (225 g) ramen, lo mein, or spaghetti
  • 1/3 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar (or lime juice)
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder (or 1 clove minced fresh garlic)
  • 2–4 tbsp warm water
  • 1/2 cup cucumber, thinly sliced or julienned

Optional Garnishes

  • Sesame seeds
  • Sliced green onions
  • Chili flakes
  • Crushed peanuts

Instructions

1. Cook the Noodles

Bring a pot of water to a boil and cook the noodles according to the package directions.

Drain and rinse under cool water if serving chilled, or keep warm if serving immediately.

2. Make the Peanut Sauce

In a medium bowl, whisk together the peanut butter, soy sauce, honey or maple syrup, sesame oil, rice vinegar, and garlic powder.

Whisk in the warm water 1 tbsp at a time until the sauce is smooth and pourable.

3. Toss with Chicken

Add the drained noodles and shredded or diced chicken directly to the bowl with the peanut sauce.

Toss thoroughly with tongs until the noodles and chicken are evenly coated with the sauce.

4. Top & Serve

Divide the noodles between bowls and top with the sliced cucumber. Finish with sesame seeds, sliced green onions, chili flakes, or crushed peanuts, if desired.

Serve warm, at room temperature, or chilled.
