Velouté Sauce

A classic French sauce made with a blonde roux and warm white stock, perfect on its own or as a base for derivative sauces.

← Back to Home
Prep: 5 min Cook: 20–25 min Yield: ~2 cups Classic Sauce

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp (45 g) unsalted butter
  • 3 tbsp (30 g) all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups (480 mL) warm white stock (chicken, veal, or fish)
  • Salt and white pepper, to taste

Instructions

1. Warm the Stock

Heat your chosen stock in a small saucepan over low heat until warm. Keep warm nearby.

2. Make the Blonde Roux

Melt the butter in a heavy-bottomed saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the flour and whisk continuously for 2–3 minutes, until pale golden and fragrant, forming a blonde roux.

3. Incorporate the Stock

Slowly pour in the warm stock in a steady stream while whisking vigorously to prevent lumps.

4. Simmer & Reduce

Bring the sauce to a gentle simmer over medium heat. Reduce the heat to low and cook gently for 15–20 minutes, stirring frequently, until smooth and thickened enough to coat the back of a spoon.

5. Season & Finish

Remove from the heat. Season with salt and freshly ground white pepper to taste.

Serve immediately, or use as a base for derivative sauces.
