1-Pot Creamy Tuscan Chicken & Penne

A creamy one-pot pasta with golden chicken, sun-dried tomatoes, spinach, Parmesan, and tender penne.

← Back to Home
Prep: 10 min Cook: 20–25 min Serves: 3–4 One-Pot Meal

Ingredients

  • 450 g boneless, skinless chicken breasts, sliced into bite-sized strips
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 115 g sun-dried tomatoes, drained and chopped
  • 225 g penne or rigatoni pasta, uncooked
  • 2 1/2 cups chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup milk or half-and-half
  • 60 g fresh spinach
  • 50 g Parmesan cheese, freshly grated
  • Salt, to taste
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • Italian seasoning, to taste

Instructions

1. Sear the Chicken

Heat the olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Season the sliced chicken with salt, black pepper, and Italian seasoning.

Sear for 5–6 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and golden. Transfer the chicken to a plate and set aside.

2. Sauté the Aromatics

Reduce the heat to medium. Add the butter, minced garlic, and chopped sun-dried tomatoes to the remaining chicken drippings.

Sauté for 1–2 minutes, until fragrant.

3. Cook the Pasta

Pour in the chicken broth and milk or half-and-half. Add the uncooked penne and stir to combine.

Bring to a boil, then cover with the Dutch oven lid and reduce the heat to low. Simmer for 10–12 minutes, stirring occasionally so the pasta does not stick to the bottom, until the pasta is al dente.

4. Finish the Sauce

Remove the lid and stir in the Parmesan cheese, fresh spinach, and cooked chicken.

Stir continuously for 1–2 minutes, until the spinach wilts and the remaining liquid thickens into a glossy, creamy sauce.

Taste and adjust the salt, black pepper, and Italian seasoning as needed. Serve immediately.
