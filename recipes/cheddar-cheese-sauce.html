Cheddar Cheese Sauce

A rich and creamy Béchamel-based cheese sauce made with sharp Cheddar, mustard, Worcestershire, and a touch of cayenne.

← Back to Home
Prep: 5 min Cook: 5 min Yield: ~2 1/2 cups Cheese Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1 recipe prepared Béchamel Sauce, hot
  • 1 1/2 cups (150 g) sharp Cheddar cheese, shredded
  • 1/2 tsp English mustard powder (or Dijon mustard)
  • 1/2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • Pinch of cayenne pepper or paprika

Instructions

1. Prepare the Base

Prepare the standard Béchamel Sauce and remove the saucepan from the heat.

2. Add the Seasonings

Whisk the mustard powder, Worcestershire sauce, and cayenne pepper or paprika into the hot sauce until evenly combined.

3. Melt the Cheese

Add the shredded Cheddar in small batches, stirring gently after each addition until completely melted and smooth.

Important: Do not boil the sauce after adding the cheese, as excessive heat can cause the cheese to separate and the sauce to become grainy.

4. Finish & Serve

Taste and adjust the salt and pepper if needed. Serve immediately while hot and creamy.

Excellent over vegetables, pasta, baked potatoes, or anywhere a creamy Cheddar sauce is needed.
