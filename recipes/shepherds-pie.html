Shepherd's Pie

A simple family-style shepherd's pie layered with seasoned ground beef, creamy corn, and buttery mashed potatoes.

← Back to Home
Prep: 20 min Bake: 25–30 min Rest: 5 min Comfort Food

Ingredients

Beef & Corn

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tbsp Montreal steak spice
  • 2 small cans (about 10 oz / 284 mL each) creamed corn

Mashed Potatoes

  • 1.5 lbs yellow or Russet potatoes (about 3–4 medium)
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup milk or cream
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Instructions

1. Boil the Potatoes

Peel and cube the potatoes. Place them in a pot and cover with cold, salted water. Bring to a boil and cook for 12–15 minutes, or until fork-tender.

Drain well, then mash with the butter and milk or cream. Season with salt and pepper and mash until smooth.

2. Cook the Meat

Heat a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chopped onion and cook for 3–5 minutes, until softened.

Add the ground beef and Montreal steak spice. Cook until the beef is completely browned, breaking it apart as it cooks. Drain excess fat if needed.

3. Assemble

Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).

Layer the ingredients in a 9x6-inch baking dish in this order:

  1. Bottom: Spread the browned ground beef mixture evenly in the dish.
  2. Middle: Spread the 2 cans of creamed corn evenly over the beef.
  3. Top: Spread the mashed potatoes over the corn. Rough up the surface with a fork to create crispy peaks.

4. Bake

Bake uncovered at 375°F (190°C) for 25–30 minutes.

Broil on high for an additional 2–3 minutes, or until the mashed potato topping is golden brown. Watch closely while broiling.

Let the shepherd's pie rest for 5 minutes before serving.
