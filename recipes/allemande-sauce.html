Allemande Sauce (with Milk & Butter)

A rich Velouté-based sauce finished with an egg yolk and milk liaison, lemon, butter, and a touch of nutmeg.

← Back to Home
Prep: 5 min Cook: 5 min Yield: ~2 1/2 cups Velouté-Based Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1 recipe prepared Velouté Sauce (made with chicken or veal stock), hot
  • 2 large egg yolks
  • 7 tbsp whole milk
  • 1 tbsp (15 g) unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 tsp–1 tbsp fresh lemon juice, to taste
  • 1 tbsp (15 g) cold unsalted butter, for finishing
  • Pinch of freshly grated nutmeg
  • Salt and white pepper, to taste

Instructions

1. Whisk the Liaison

In a medium mixing bowl, whisk together the egg yolks, whole milk, and 1 tbsp melted butter until completely smooth and combined.

2. Temper the Egg Mixture

Slowly ladle about 1/2 cup of the hot Velouté sauce into the milk and egg mixture while whisking constantly. This gradually warms the egg yolks without scrambling them.

3. Incorporate & Heat Gently

Reduce the stove heat to very low. Slowly pour the tempered egg mixture back into the saucepan containing the remaining hot Velouté, whisking constantly.

4. Thicken — Do Not Boil

Cook over low heat for 1–2 minutes, stirring constantly, until the sauce thickens and coats the back of a spoon.

Important: Do not allow the sauce to boil after adding the egg yolks, or the eggs may curdle.

5. Finish & Season

Remove from the heat. Whisk in the lemon juice, cold butter, and nutmeg until the butter is completely incorporated and the sauce is smooth.

Season with salt and white pepper to taste. Serve immediately.
