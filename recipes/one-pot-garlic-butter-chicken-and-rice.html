One-Pot Garlic Butter Chicken and Rice

A rich, comforting one-pot meal with golden chicken and tender rice cooked directly in garlic butter, seasoned chicken drippings, and broth.

← Back to Home
Prep: 10 min Cook: 40–45 min Serves: 4 One-Pot Meal

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (or breasts)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup long-grain white rice, rinsed
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning (or thyme/rosemary)
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Instructions

1. Sear the Chicken

Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).

Season the chicken generously with salt, black pepper, and Italian seasoning. Heat the olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat.

Place the chicken skin-side down and sear for 5–6 minutes, until deeply golden. Flip and cook for another 2 minutes, then transfer the chicken to a plate.

2. Sauté the Aromatics & Toast the Rice

Reduce the heat to medium and melt the butter in the remaining chicken drippings.

Add the diced onion and sauté for 3 minutes, until translucent.

Stir in the minced garlic and rinsed rice. Cook for 1–2 minutes, stirring frequently, to lightly toast the rice.

3. Deglaze & Assemble

Pour in the chicken broth, scraping the bottom of the Dutch oven to release the browned bits.

Bring the liquid to a simmer, then place the seared chicken on top of the rice with the skin-side facing up.

4. Bake Until Tender

Cover the Dutch oven with its lid and transfer to the preheated oven.

Bake for 25–30 minutes, or until the rice is tender and the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C).

Let rest for 5 minutes before serving.
