Cucumber & Pickled Red Onion Couscous Salad

A light and refreshing couscous salad with crisp cucumber, tangy pickled red onions, fresh herbs, and a simple olive oil dressing.

← Back to Home
Prep: 10 min Rest: 5 min Serves: 3–4 Side Dish

Ingredients

Couscous

  • 1 cup plain couscous
  • 1 cup hot chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Salad

  • 1/2 English cucumber, finely diced
  • 1/4 cup pickled red onions, roughly chopped
  • 1 tbsp pickled red onion liquid
  • 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice (optional, adjust to taste)
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley or mint, chopped (optional)
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Instructions

1. Prepare the Couscous

Place the plain couscous in a medium mixing bowl.

Pour the hot chicken or vegetable broth over the couscous and add 1 tbsp olive oil.

Stir briefly to combine, then immediately cover the bowl with a plate or lid.

Let sit undisturbed for 5 minutes, allowing the couscous to absorb the broth.

2. Fluff & Cool

Remove the cover and fluff the couscous thoroughly with a fork to separate the grains.

Allow the couscous to cool for approximately 5 minutes before adding the vegetables.

This helps keep the cucumber crisp and prevents the vegetables from becoming steamed or softened.

3. Add the Vegetables & Herbs

Add the finely diced cucumber and chopped pickled red onions to the couscous.

Sprinkle in the chopped parsley or mint, if using.

Gently fold everything together until the ingredients are evenly distributed.

4. Dress & Season

Drizzle the extra-virgin olive oil and 1 tbsp pickled red onion liquid over the salad.

Add the optional fresh lemon juice for extra brightness and acidity.

Toss gently until the couscous and vegetables are evenly coated.

Season with salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste.

5. Serve

Serve warm or at room temperature.

Fluff gently with a fork before serving to keep the couscous light and separated.

Chef's Tip

This salad makes an excellent accompaniment to sticky pork strips or other richly seasoned meats.

The crisp cucumber and tangy pickled onions provide a refreshing contrast to sweet and savory sauces, while the couscous absorbs any extra sauce left on the plate.

Tips

  • For extra tanginess: Add a little more pickled onion liquid or fresh lemon juice to taste.
  • For a fresher flavor: Use chopped mint or parsley just before serving.
  • For fluffy couscous: Avoid stirring while it absorbs the broth. Fluff thoroughly with a fork afterward.
  • For a chilled salad: Refrigerate after preparing and toss gently before serving.
  • Make ahead: Prepare the couscous in advance and add the cucumber shortly before serving for the best texture.
