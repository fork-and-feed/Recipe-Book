Dutch Oven Pork Tenderloin

Juicy seared pork tenderloin roasted with baby potatoes, carrots, and onions in a savory Dijon pan sauce.

← Back to Home
Prep: 15 min Cook: 30–35 min Serves: 3–4 Oven: 400°F

Ingredients

  • 1 pork tenderloin, about 1–1 1/4 lb
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 medium onion, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 2 carrots, cut into chunks
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp butter, optional for finishing

Instructions

1. Season the Pork

Preheat the oven to 400°F (205°C). Heat a Dutch oven over medium-high heat.

Pat the pork tenderloin dry and season generously with salt, black pepper, paprika, and thyme.

2. Sear the Pork

Add the oil to the Dutch oven. Sear the pork for 2–3 minutes per side, until nicely browned. Transfer to a plate and set aside.

3. Cook the Onion

Reduce the heat to medium. Add 1 tbsp butter and the sliced onion. Cook for 4–5 minutes, until the onion begins to soften and brown.

4. Add the Garlic

Add the minced garlic and cook for 30 seconds, until fragrant.

5. Add the Flour

Sprinkle the flour over the onions and stir continuously for about 1 minute.

6. Build the Sauce

Slowly pour in the chicken broth, stirring and scraping up the browned bits from the bottom of the Dutch oven.

Stir in the Dijon mustard and Worcestershire sauce until combined.

7. Add the Vegetables

Add the halved baby potatoes and carrots to the sauce. Nestle the seared pork tenderloin on top of the vegetables.

8. Cover & Roast

Cover the Dutch oven and transfer it to the preheated oven. Roast at 400°F (205°C) for about 15 minutes.

9. Finish Uncovered

Remove the lid and continue roasting for another 5–10 minutes, or until the thickest part of the pork reaches an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C).

10. Rest the Pork & Finish the Sauce

Transfer the pork to a cutting board and let it rest for 5–10 minutes.

If the sauce is too thin, place the Dutch oven over medium heat and simmer uncovered for a few minutes until it reaches your desired consistency.

For a richer finish, stir in the optional tablespoon of butter.

11. Serve

Slice the rested pork tenderloin and serve over the potatoes, carrots, and onions with plenty of the Dijon pan sauce spooned over the top.

Important

Pork tenderloin is very lean. Cook it to an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C) to keep it juicy rather than cooking it to the higher temperatures commonly used for tougher cuts such as pork shoulder.
