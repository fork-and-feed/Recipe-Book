French Onion Egg Noodles

Creamy egg noodles with deeply caramelized onions, garlic, thyme, Dijon, and a silky French onion-inspired sauce.

← Back to Home
Prep: 15 min Cook: 40 min Serves: 4 Creamy Pasta

Ingredients

  • 300–350 g egg noodles
  • 2 medium yellow onions, thinly sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 tsp apple cider vinegar mixed with 2 tbsp water
  • 3 tbsp butter, divided
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 400 mL whole milk, divided
  • 1 1/2–2 tsp Dijon mustard, to taste
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 tsp freshly cracked black pepper
  • 1/2 cup freshly grated Gruyère, Fontina, or Parmesan (optional)
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley or chives, finely chopped (for garnish)

Instructions

1. Caramelize the Onions

Melt 1 1/2 tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium-low heat. Add the sliced onions and a pinch of salt. Cook for 25–35 minutes, stirring occasionally, until deeply golden brown and very soft. Add 1 tbsp water if the onions begin to stick. Stir in the fresh thyme during the final 5 minutes.

2. Add Garlic & Deglaze

Stir in the finely chopped garlic and cook for 2 minutes until fragrant and golden. Pour in the apple cider vinegar and water mixture, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the skillet. Simmer for about 30 seconds, until the liquid is almost completely evaporated.

3. Blend for Texture

Transfer about 2/3 of the onion and garlic mixture to a blender. Add 100 mL of the milk and blend until completely smooth. Transfer the remaining 1/3 of the caramelized onions to a bowl and set aside.

4. Make the Roux

Melt the remaining 1 1/2 tbsp butter in the same skillet over medium-low heat. Whisk in the flour and cook for 1 minute to remove the raw flour taste.

5. Build the Cream Base

Gradually whisk in the remaining 300 mL milk. Cook for 4–5 minutes, whisking frequently, until the mixture is smooth and thickened.

6. Combine & Season

Stir in the blended onion puree, reserved caramelized onions, Dijon mustard, Worcestershire sauce, black pepper, and salt. Simmer gently over low heat for 3 minutes.

Optional: Remove from the heat and stir in the grated Gruyère, Fontina, or Parmesan until completely melted and smooth.

7. Boil Noodles & Assemble

Cook the egg noodles in salted boiling water until just tender. Before draining, reserve 1/2 cup of hot pasta water.

Drain the noodles and toss them directly into the warm sauce. Add reserved pasta water 1–2 tbsp at a time until the sauce reaches a glossy, silky consistency that clings to the noodles.

8. Finish

Taste and adjust the salt, pepper, or Dijon as needed. Garnish with finely chopped fresh parsley or chives and serve immediately.
