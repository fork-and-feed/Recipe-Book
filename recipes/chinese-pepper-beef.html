Chinese Pepper Beef

High-heat stir-fry with tender beef, crisp peppers, and a glossy black pepper sauce.

← Back to Home
Prep: 10–15 min Cook: 10 min Total: ~25 min Serves: 3–4

Ingredients

Beef

  • 1 lb thinly sliced beef
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp cornstarch
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Black pepper, to taste

Vegetables

  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • Optional: mushrooms

Sauce

  • 1/3 cup beef broth or water
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp dark soy sauce (optional)
  • 1 tsp sugar or honey
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 tsp cornstarch

Optional Additions

  • Chili flakes or chili crisp
  • Green onions
  • Sesame seeds

Instructions

  1. Marinate beef: Toss beef with soy sauce, cornstarch, oil, and black pepper. Let sit 10–15 minutes.
  2. Mix sauce: Whisk all sauce ingredients in a bowl until smooth.
  3. Heat pan: Heat a wok or skillet over high heat until very hot.
  4. Sear beef: Sear beef in batches for 1–2 minutes total. Remove before fully cooked.
  5. Stir-fry veg: Add a bit more oil if needed. Cook onions and peppers 3–5 minutes until slightly blistered but still crisp.
  6. Add garlic: Stir in garlic and cook 30 seconds.
  7. Thicken: Stir sauce again (cornstarch settles), pour into pan, and simmer 30–60 seconds until thickened and glossy.
  8. Finish: Return beef to pan and toss 1 minute until coated and heated through.
  9. Serve: Serve over rice or noodles. Add optional toppings if you like.

Notes

  • High heat + quick cooking keeps the peppers crisp and the beef tender.
  • For extra pepper punch, add more freshly cracked black pepper at the end.
