1-Pot Baked Sausage Penne

A hearty one-pot pasta with browned Italian sausage, marinara, tender penne, and a bubbling layer of melted mozzarella.

← Back to Home
Prep: 10 min Cook: 30 min Serves: 4–6 One-Pot Meal

Ingredients

  • 450 g Italian sausage (sweet or hot, casings removed)
  • 600 mL marinara sauce
  • 3 cups beef broth (or chicken broth)
  • 340 g penne pasta, uncooked
  • 225 g mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, to taste
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • Italian seasoning, to taste

Instructions

1. Brown the Sausage

Heat the olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the sausage and cook for 6–8 minutes, breaking it into crumbles as it cooks, until browned.

Drain excess fat if desired.

2. Add the Pasta & Liquid

Pour in the marinara sauce and broth. Add the uncooked penne and stir well to combine, making sure the pasta is submerged in the liquid.

3. Simmer

Bring the mixture to a boil over high heat. Cover with the Dutch oven lid, reduce the heat to low, and simmer for 10–12 minutes.

Stir once halfway through cooking to prevent the pasta from sticking. Continue cooking until the penne is al dente.

4. Add the Cheese & Bake

Preheat the oven to 400°F.

Remove the lid and scatter the shredded mozzarella evenly over the pasta.

Transfer the Dutch oven, uncovered, to the preheated oven and bake for 10 minutes, or until the cheese is completely melted and bubbling.

For a faster finish, broil for 2–3 minutes instead, watching closely to prevent burning.
