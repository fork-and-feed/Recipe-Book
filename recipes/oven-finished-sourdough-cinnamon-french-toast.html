Oven-Finished Sourdough Cinnamon French Toast

Thick sourdough French toast seared until golden, sprinkled with cinnamon sugar, and finished in the oven for a soft, custardy centre.

← Back to Home
Prep: 5 min Cook: 10–15 min Serves: 2 Makes: 4 slices

Ingredients

  • 4 thick slices sourdough bread
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 3/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/8 tsp salt
  • 1–2 tbsp butter, for cooking

Cinnamon Sugar

  • 1 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven: Preheat to 375°F (190°C). Place a baking sheet in the oven while it preheats.
  2. Make the custard: In a shallow bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, brown sugar, cinnamon, vanilla, and salt.
  3. Make the cinnamon sugar: In a small bowl, mix together the granulated sugar and cinnamon. Set aside.
  4. Soak the bread: Dip each slice of sourdough into the egg mixture. Let thick or slightly stale bread soak for about 20–30 seconds per side. Fresh bread only needs about 10–15 seconds per side.
  5. Heat the skillet: Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat.
  6. Brown the French toast: Cook the soaked bread for about 2 minutes per side, until golden brown. The centre does not need to be fully cooked yet.
  7. Transfer to the oven: Carefully remove the hot baking sheet from the oven. Lightly butter the sheet and arrange the French toast in a single layer.
  8. Add the cinnamon sugar: Sprinkle the tops evenly with the cinnamon sugar.
  9. Finish baking: Bake for 6–10 minutes, depending on the thickness of the bread, until the centres are cooked through but still soft and custardy.
  10. Serve: Serve immediately with butter and maple syrup.
