BBQ Chicken Salad Sandwiches

An easy chicken salad made from a store-bought BBQ chicken with crunchy celery, sweet pickles, and a creamy dressing.

← Back to Home
Prep: 15 min Chill: 30 min Makes: 8–10 sandwiches No Cooking

Ingredients

  • Meat from 1 whole store-bought BBQ chicken, skin and bones removed (about 4–5 cups chopped or shredded)
  • 3/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp sour cream or plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 celery stalks, finely diced
  • 1/2 cup finely chopped sweet pickles
  • 1 tbsp sweet pickle juice
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt (or to taste)
  • 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • Bread, croissants, wraps, or buns
  • Lettuce (optional)

Instructions

  1. Remove the skin and bones from the BBQ chicken. Chop or shred the meat and place it in a large bowl.
  2. Add the diced celery and chopped sweet pickles.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, sour cream (or Greek yogurt), sweet pickle juice, Dijon mustard, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  4. Pour the dressing over the chicken mixture and stir until evenly coated.
  5. Taste and adjust with additional mayonnaise, pickle juice, salt, or pepper if desired.
  6. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving.
  7. Serve on bread, croissants, wraps, or buns with lettuce, if desired.

Storage

  • Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for 3–4 days.
  • Do not freeze, as the mayonnaise-based dressing may separate.
